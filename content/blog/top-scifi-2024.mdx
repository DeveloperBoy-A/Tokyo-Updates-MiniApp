## 1. Dune: Part Two

Denis Villeneuve's epic continues Paul Atreides' journey with sweeping world-building and striking visuals.

## 2. Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga

A brutal wasteland saga with inventive action and memorable production design.

## 3. The Substance

A bold body-horror satire that pushes its premise into surreal territory.

## 4. Alien: Romulus

A return to claustrophobic franchise horror with tense set pieces.

## 5. Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes

A new chapter that respects the legacy while opening a fresh world.

## More notable releases

6. **A Quiet Place: Day One** — survival tension in an apocalyptic world.  
7. **Inside Out 2** — imaginative and science-fiction-adjacent.  
8. **Megalopolis** — an ambitious vision of the future.  
9. **Venom: The Last Dance** — comic-book spectacle with sci-fi action.  
10. **Subservience** — an AI-focused thriller about trust and control.
